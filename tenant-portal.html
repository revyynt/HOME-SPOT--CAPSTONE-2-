Please review and accept your building policies & lease terms

Before proceeding to your Tenant Portal, please confirm that you understand and agree to adhere to MJP Residences rules and contract terms:

1. Lease Contract Duration & Move-in Fees

  • 6 Months Contract: Minimum lease duration of six (6) months.
  • Move-in Fees: 1 Month Advance Rent (₱10,000) & 2 Months Security Deposit (₱20,000).
  • Monthly Due Date: Payable every 5th of each month.

2. Mandatory Building House Rules

  • Strictly No Pets: Pets of any kind are strictly prohibited within the apartment building.
  • No Car Parking: There is strictly no car parking on-site (commute or motorcycle only).
  • Occupancy Limit: Maximum of three (3) occupants allowed per unit (3 pax maximum).
